# Library audit — `Downloads/books/`

Reviewed against the rebuilt 120-week Putnam-only plan (no AMC, no AIME).

Three questions decide every file:

1. **Does it have solutions?** Without a coach until week 44, a source you cannot grade
   yourself against is a source you cannot learn from. Problems-only compilations are
   timed-set fodder, never scheduled reading.
2. **Is it Putnam-shaped?** Putnam is 12 proof problems in 6 hours. Graduate course texts
   and research monographs are not training, they are procrastination that feels rigorous.
3. **Is it scheduled?** If no week in the plan reads it, it is a drill bank at best.

## Add — the gaps

| Book | Cost | Why |

## Free, no download needed

- [Kedlaya's Putnam Archive](https://kskedlaya.org/putnam-archive/) — every paper and official solution, 1985→present. This is your simulation supply for all 120 weeks.
- [Yufei Zhao's MIT 18.A34 Putnam Seminar](https://yufeizhao.com/a34/) — themed weekly problem sets with solutions.
- [Stanford Putnam problem sets](https://web.stanford.edu/~cm5/putnam.html) — topic-sorted, use as Block-B swaps.
- [Evan Chen's handouts and Napkin](https://web.evanchen.cc/olympiad.html) — inequality and functional-equation technique sheets.

## Video — watched AFTER an attempt, never instead of one

- **Michael Penn** — the largest catalogue of worked Putnam problems anywhere. Search the problem code ("Putnam 2016 B4") after you have already failed at it honestly.
- **Mohamed Omar** — Putnam walkthroughs that explain *why* a solver picks an approach, which is the part books skip.
- **3Blue1Brown — Essence of Linear Algebra** — watch the whole series in week 37, the week before Axler starts. Three hours that make chapters 1–5 land twice as fast. *Essence of Calculus* is an optional week-8 primer.
- **Richard Borcherds** — group theory and Galois lectures for week 54.
- **MIT OCW 18.100A** (analysis) and **18.06 Strang** (linear algebra) — a second voice when a Rudin or Axler chapter stalls for more than two days.
- **blackpenredpen** — integration and limit mechanics. Background noise, not training.

## Method — the multiplier nobody buys

- **Anki, 10 minutes daily.** Front: theorem statement. Back: the proof you must reproduce.
  The plan asks you to hold Spivak from week 8 until the exam in week 120 — 112 weeks. No
  amount of careful first reading survives that without spaced retrieval. This is now built
  into the Sunday review block and every P2–P9 slot C.
- **A LaTeX archive of your own best proof each week.** Searchable, and rewriting a proof
  for a reader is the exact skill the exam grades.
- **The gap log, kept separate from the error log.** Already in the plan, and still the most
  important file you will own: right ideas you under-justified are what actually cost Putnam points.
